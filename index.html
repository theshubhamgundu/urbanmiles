ABOUT US
MORE THAN A
RUN CLUB.
A LIFESTYLE.

Urban Miles is Hyderabad's community-first running experience — where the discipline of early morning runs meets the energy of music, coffee, and real human connection.

Every Sunday at 6AM, we gather. We run together, fuel up at the city's best cafés, and vibe. No pressure, no pace requirements — just people who show up.

🏃
Group Running
Fun and energetic runs designed for all fitness levels. Run together, stay motivated, enjoy every step.
🎵
Music & Vibes
Music-powered sessions that boost energy, mood, and the overall experience of every run.
☕
Post-Run Culture
Wind down at partner cafés, build friendships, and make Sunday mornings the best part of your week.
